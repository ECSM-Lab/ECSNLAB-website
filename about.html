About

About the Lab

Energy systems modeling, climate policy, and decarbonization pathways at PUCV.

Mission

Generate rigorous scientific knowledge to accelerate the global energy transition, integrating advanced mathematical modeling, climate policy analysis, and sustainable development frameworks to inform real-world decision-making.

Vision

To become the leading research hub in the Southern Hemisphere for integrated energy–climate–economy modeling, empowering the next generation of engineers and policymakers to design evidence-based, equitable decarbonization pathways.

What sets us apart. The lab combines operations-research rigor with direct policy engagement. Our work feeds into the IPCC, APEC, the Ministry of Energy and the Ministry of Environment of Chile, and a wide network of international partners.

What we do

The lab develops mathematical and policy models to study how energy systems can decarbonize at the speed required by the Paris Agreement, while remaining technically feasible, economically efficient, and socially just. Our toolkit spans linear and mixed-integer programming, stochastic and bilevel optimization, mixed-complementarity equilibrium models, and integrated-assessment models such as GCAM. We are committed to open science and to training the next generation of energy and climate researchers.

We collaborate closely with national and international institutions including the IPCC, APEC, the U.S. Department of Energy / PNNL, the SDEWES Centre, and a network of universities across the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.
